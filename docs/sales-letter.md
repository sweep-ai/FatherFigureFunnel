## Hook

You don't need a six-pack.

You need to be the guy your kids think you are.

Here's a question nobody asks you. You're pumping gas. Wife and kids are in the car. Something goes sideways at the next pump. Could you handle it? Not "could you call someone." Could *you* handle it?

If you had to think about that for even a second — keep watching.

## The moment

I'm Coach John. I'm a dad. My son plays travel hockey, my daughter dances competitively, and most weeks my wife and I are running a divide-and-conquer schedule that would break most people.

I've also spent years running a gym in the Twin Cities and coaching hundreds of people through their comebacks. And the guy who walks in the door looking for help almost always says the same thing:

*"I used to be in pretty good shape. What happened?"*

Here's what happened. You became the guy everybody leans on. At work, you're the one who fixes the problem. At home, you're the one who keeps the wheels on. You take care of everyone and everything — except you.

So your family gets what's left of you. You're short with the kids at 7 p.m. You're asleep on the couch by 9. You catch yourself in the bathroom mirror brushing your teeth with no shirt on and you don't recognize the guy looking back.

## The broken promise

Think about how many times you've said it.

"Monday, I'm starting."
"Tomorrow I'm getting up early."
"This month I'm losing the weight."
"I'm done drinking during the week."

And then you don't. Nobody else even knows you broke the promise. But you do.

And after enough Mondays, something worse than the weight happens: **you stop trusting your own word.**

That's the real problem. Not a lack of information. You know what a squat is. Knowledge was never your problem. *Follow-through* is — because every plan you've tried was built for a 24-year-old with no kids and nowhere to be.

## The mechanism — Faith. Family. Fitness.

The Father Figure Formula isn't a workout plan. It's a comeback built on three pillars:

- **Fitness is the vehicle.** Three full-body sessions a week, built around six movements — squat, hinge, lunge, push, pull, carry. No more than 45 minutes. Nutrition that fits a family dinner table.
- **Family is the reason.** Every goal ties back to the man you want your kids to remember — the dad who actually did things with them.
- **Faith is the foundation.** Purpose, values, and the discipline to keep small promises to yourself. One promise for the next 24 hours. Keep it. Then do it again.

You don't win 30 pounds today. You win *the day*. Get your protein. Take your walk. Train when you said you would. One kept promise at a time, you become a man whose word means something again — especially to himself.

## The coaching (what you actually get)

1. **1:1 onboarding call + intake** — your schedule, your body, your history, your family.
2. **Custom training + nutrition** built around your real week — travel, kids' practices, late meetings.
3. **Direct access to your coach** — message us when you're stuck, not just at check-in.
4. **Weekly 1:1 check-ins** — we adjust the plan to how you're actually doing.
5. **The brotherhood** — group calls and a community of dads doing the same work.
